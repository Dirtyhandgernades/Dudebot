## P0-1 — prevent premature vendor daily bars from appearing complete

The bounded vendor diagnostic previously accepted a successful daily OHLC bar for the requested session regardless of whether the HTTP request began before that regular session had closed. A same-day vendor daily bar requested at 15:59 ET could be a mutable partial bar, and response completion after 16:00 ET must not turn it into a completed-session observation.

Research-only change to `backtest/obsidian_two_provider_close_reconciliation.py`: record a local UTC `requested_at` **before** opening the HTTP request, retain the response retrieval timestamp separately, and fail closed in `observation()` if request-start time is missing, invalid, timezone-naive, or not strictly later than 16:00 `America/New_York` on the requested session date. Blocked observations use `SESSION_NOT_COMPLETE_AT_REQUEST_OR_TIMESTAMP_UNVERIFIED`, retain the complete response digest if present, but no price or vendor-bar validity. The conservative 16:00 ET cutoff handles DST (Oct27 20:00Z vs Nov2 21:00Z); **it does not validate exchange holidays, special sessions, early closes, official auction price provenance, corporate actions, or independent underlying feed ownership.** A post-cutoff result is only `VALID_VENDOR_BAR`, never an exchange-official close.

Two new regressions test preclose/boundary/postclose request starts across EDT/EST, malformed/naive/missing timestamps, a response completed after cutoff from a preclose request, and vendor-supplied timestamps that cannot replace local request-start evidence. Existing test fixtures now explicitly carry local request-start timestamps.
