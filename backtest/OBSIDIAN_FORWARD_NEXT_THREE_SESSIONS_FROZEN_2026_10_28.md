# OBSIDIAN — Next 1–3 real trading sessions, independent forward shadow policy v1

**Frozen at engineering creation Oct 28, 2026, on research-only branch.** This is separate from the Oct27 frozen same-day policy and not a retrospective revision. All records are RESEARCH_ONLY_NOT_AN_ORDER.

- Observation cohort: original authenticated **MORNING** or **INTRADAY** single-venue IEX capture, original immutable universe, original price-only early/intermediate watch statuses. Preserves every unselected, blocked, stale, no-watch symbol.
- Independent study horizon: original session close h0 followed by its **next three actual exchange sessions h1, h2, h3** (not weekdays or calendar days). Require supplied, exchange-source-dated session chain; only normal 16:00 ET close for v1. Holidays/early closes must not be guessed.
- Target: **first completed regular-session official-close crossing <=80% of the original independently verified prior-session regular close** across h0–h3. If h0 already met target, classify separately as same-day and never recount h1–h3 as future prediction wins.
- Provisional early criterion at first *future* crossing: original watch existed; independently supplied IEX observation as-of was before close; `(IEX decision price - future official close)/(original prior official close - future official close) >= 0.50`. Otherwise label late or unwatched. This is a timing comparison, not an executable short.
- Source intake: two independently verifiable provider organizations and hosts, exact 16:00 ET as-of and prior/current close evidence, all conflicting additional sources retained, independent corporate-action/unchanged-security review for *each* interval, complete ordered calendar and inter-day price chain. Missing prior days censor subsequent days; do not select future winners and discard unscored others.
- The code is **offline structural verification only**. User-provided 'verified:true', URL, provider group, and calendar source fields are NOT independently authenticated in this code. The module never certifies official prices, actual early prediction, probability, precision/recall or profit until outside source checks verify evidence. Preliminary internally consistent rows are labeled PROVISIONAL.
- Multiple morning/intraday captures of the same issuer/day are correlated and **must not be aggregated as independent signals**. A future session-level matched-cohort evaluator may count earliest genuinely verified observation once.
- Never mix with 2023–2025 tuned historical research or retrospective Oct26–28 winner diagnostics as prospective advantage. No rewriting original frozen policy or existing IEX artifacts.
- Unknown platform borrow, common-stock eligibility, cap/share consistency, liquidity, fills/halts and after-cost returns remain blockers to executable claims.

Implementation: `backtest/obsidian_forward_horizon_shadow.py`; isolated synthetic CI tests `tests/test_obsidian_prospective_forward_horizon_shadow.py`. CLI requires three explicit external input paths and writes a new report only once: `python -m backtest.obsidian_forward_horizon_shadow --capture CAPTURE --calendar CALENDAR.json --outcomes OUTCOMES.json --output NEW_REPORT.json`. This does **not** fetch market data or fill missing data automatically.
