# OBSIDIAN discretionary evidence-tier research protocol — frozen v1, 2026-10-26

**Research branch only; not an execution system.** This protocol is frozen as `OBSIDIAN_DISCRETIONARY_TIERS_V1_2026_10_26` prior to any forward outcomes used to evaluate it. It must not replace the historical or prospective baseline journal, trading controls, or user decisions. No live trades or orders.

## Problem
The prior all-factor mechanism classifier distinguishes complete evidence from incomplete evidence, but cannot show whether an *early, incomplete yet independently evidenced* setup should have been monitored more closely. Forcing full price confirmation can make discovery too late, while treating every decline or financing registration as a trade manufactures false positives.

## Independent tiers
- **HARD_BLOCKED:** any independently timestamped SMG/market hard gate is missing or false. Keep the underlying soft tier for *research analysis only*, never as a trade. Hard fields include common-stock type and NASDAQ/NYSE listing; current and confirmed prior-session price >=$3; current shares and >=$25M cap consistent with price; no unresolved halt/corporate action; fresh <=15-minute quote and fresh <=15-minute halt, corporate-action, liquidity, buying-power, exposure and platform-short proofs; verified exchange session open inside the EOD clock window; and actual SMG short availability and platform permission for shorts. An Alpaca shortable flag is insufficient. ADS share-ratio ambiguity blocks common-stock verification.
- **WATCH_ONLY:** hard gates pass but no independently evidenced mechanism, or incomplete thesis/entry/invalidation/exit, or exhausted B (<=-25%) without a separately verified fresh leg. A raw score, firm relationship, registration without verified saleability, or daily percentage change is never sufficient.
- **SPECULATIVE_SETUP:** hard gates pass, dated pre-decision adverse disclosure or *verified saleable* supply PLUS at least one verified weak-tape sign for SHORT, but incomplete full breakdown confirmation; for LONG C candidate, dated failed breakdown plus a verified reclaim/higher low/buying-volume sign. Requires explicit thesis, entry trigger, invalidation, exit rule and liquidity/exposure verification. Missing soft confirmations are shown, not silently vetoed. **Research-only**; not an executable recommendation, calibrated probability, or confirmed breakdown. The v1 initial-size *ceiling* is 5% of the lesser verified account equity and buying power; this is a conservative uncalibrated cap, not a recommended dollar trade or risk-free size.
- **CONFIRMED_SETUP:** hard gates and plan pass plus dated adverse mechanism and all four failed rebound/support loss/selling volume/liquidity evidence for SHORT; for LONG C all four failed-breakdown/support reclaim/higher low/buying-volume signs. **Still research-only**, not an order or proof of profitability.

## Timing and provenance
Each observation, hard fact, filing and tape proof carries its own independently supplied source identifier and timezone-aware observed-at timestamp. A filing with published-at later than the decision is excluded even if it was observed earlier. Unknown/future evidence fails closed. Quote and execution-sensitive proofs older than 15 minutes fail hard. A weekday EOD clock time is not sufficient to establish an open market session; independently verified market-session status is required inside the EOD window. After-hours observations can be classified as research but do NOT retroactively qualify the user's 14:45–14:58 Central closing decision window. No backfill of failed 2026-10-26 SIP capture (GitHub run 40389830214, provider auth 401/403, zero artifacts).

## Forward study, untouched outcomes
At each genuine decision-time checkpoint freeze full *unselected* eligible and unresolved universe, candidate/rejection states, timestamps, tier, mechanism proof, hard blockers, entry/invalidation/exit plan, and the frozen v1 policy hash. Preserve original raw evidence and missingness. Compare the existing all-factor baseline against the v1 partial-evidence challenger on the **same available decision times and eligible security universe**. No historical 2023–25 winners or the AEHL diagnostic case may be used for threshold tuning.

After independent outcome maturation, label >=20% subsequent **regular-session closing-price** declines over a preregistered identical horizon; separately label ordinary drops, after-hours-only moves and price adjustments. Compute lead time before decline, missed eligible collapses, screened but vetoed collapses, false positives, positive predictive value with denominator, adverse squeeze excursions, liquidity-adjusted fills, modeled borrow/slippage/fees, realized-after-cost simulated P&L, maximum drawdown and coverage/missingness by provider. Include non-signals and unavailable/blocked names in the denominator audit; never assume an unverified borrow was executable.

## What is not established
No authenticated 2026-10-26 prospective SIP capture, no current SMG borrow/holdings, no actual executable speculative candidate, no prospective calibration and no proven edge. Existing 2023–25 backtest outcomes were already inspected. Passing offline tests validates code behavior only.
