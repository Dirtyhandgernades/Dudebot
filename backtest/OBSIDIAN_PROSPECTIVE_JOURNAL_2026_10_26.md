# OBSIDIAN prospective point-in-time journal — frozen v1 (2026-10-26)

**Research only.** The offline `backtest/obsidian_prospective_journal.py` module records independently screened 2026+ candidate snapshots without sending orders, alerts, market requests or modifying production. It is **not** a scanner, a fill simulator, an executable trading recommendation, or a demonstrated edge. The frozen diagnostic `-2%` prior-close change is unchanged and must **not** be tuned using the inspected 2023–2025 outcomes.

## Procedure

1. Before looking at the next-session outcome, run an **independent full-market scan** and preserve **every screened candidate** in JSONL, including A/B/C category, LONG/SHORT direction, screen identifier, and candidates with unknown market cap, listing, halt, borrow or SMG platform availability. Never submit only eventual winners or triggered signals. Keep independent scan coverage and the screened universe denominator separately; this journal **cannot prove full-market recall**.
2. Record `session_date` (Central calendar date), `decision_at` (timezone-aware ISO 8601), `ticker`, `direction`, `category`, `screen_id`, `exchange`, `security_type`, `halted`, `corporate_actions_clear`, `decision_price`, `previous_close`, `shares_outstanding`, `cutoff_at` and `last_complete_at`. The permitted window is **2:45–2:58 PM America/Chicago** (3:45–3:58 PM Eastern during both standard and daylight time). Inputs from outside the window are recorded but blocked.
3. For each evidence family `screen`, `quote`, `previous_close`, `shares`, `listing`, `corporate_actions`, and for shorts `borrow` and `smg_short` (for longs `smg_long`), provide **three fields**: `<family>_verified: true`, `<family>_url: "https://..."`, `<family>_at: "2026-10-26T14:49:00-05:00"`. Unknown evidence should be recorded as unknown, not filled by assumption. Source URLs and boolean attestations must be independently checked; the journal cannot authenticate a claim simply because fields say `true`.
4. For SHORT, record `borrow_available` and `smg_short_available` as explicit booleans only when separately verified; for LONG use `smg_long_available`. Quote, borrow and SMG availability must be timestamped within five minutes of decision. Only NYSE/Nasdaq common shares, current/prior prices >=$3, estimated cap >=$25M from as-of shares, and resolved corporate actions/halts pass the research gate. A passed gate is still labeled **NOT EXECUTABLE**.
5. Execute `python -m backtest.obsidian_prospective_journal --input /path/to/screened.jsonl --output /path/to/new-journal.jsonl`. Output is **write-once**: a pre-existing destination raises `FileExistsError`. Every input row is retained as `as_screened` with a separate derived `audit`; duplicate screen/ticker/direction/decision keys or future-dated evidence fail closed.
6. Only after the next session, separately join independently observed outcomes to the frozen candidate journal. Preserve unmatched, halted, missing and unknown-borrow candidates in the denominator. Compare frozen hybrid baseline and frozen early-weakness challenger on genuinely untouched sessions, and report gross/net results, execution coverage, adverse squeezes, cost sensitivity, risk limits and drawdowns. **No future close, later filing, or later short availability may enter the candidate snapshot.**

## What this does NOT solve

The journal **does not collect or authenticate independent market screens**. It has no real 2026 candidate records until an external research-only screening process supplies them. It cannot prove historical borrow availability, SMG account acceptance, actual order fills, share-count accuracy or a tradable advantage. It deliberately outputs `trade_authorized=false` even for research-eligible rows. Do not use synthetic test fixtures as real market evidence. The prior historical data recovery remains **4,731 windows checked / 2,472 timely / 2,259 empty or stale, zero original gap repairs confirmed**.

Validation: nine synthetic unit tests in `tests/test_obsidian_prospective_journal.py`; isolated CI workflow `.github/workflows/obsidian-prospective-journal-tests.yml` does not invoke the completed historical recovery.
